# What are FullOn account types?

The official FAQ lists:

- Native & sovereign accounts.
- Proxy accounts.
- Social-recoverable accounts.
- Simple address accounts, with WASM-managed and EVM-managed variants.

The original page provides categories and subheadings, not complete permission, registration, or live-network details. Labels alone do not establish exclusive owner control, free registration, or guaranteed recovery.

Read [account permissions](../01_Core_Chain/accounts.md) and the [reviewed RWID implementation](../01_Core_Chain/rwid-accounts.md) for verified mechanisms.
