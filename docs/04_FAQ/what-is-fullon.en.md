# What is FullOn?

The official FAQ describes FullOn as a decentralized Layer 1 blockchain focused on scalability, low latency, fast finality, and multi-chain interoperability.

This is product positioning, not a measured TPS or finality-latency report. Continue with [accounts](../01_Core_Chain/accounts.md), [RWID](../01_Core_Chain/rwid-accounts.md), and [specifications](../01_Core_Chain/specifications.md).
