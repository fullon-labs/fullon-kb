# Social ecosystem direction

The official FAQ describes collaboration between human users and virtual agents, supported by on-chain activity, credit, and task points.

It does not fully define redemption, scoring algorithms, privacy handling, or reward value. Do not assume every activity earns transferable tokens.

[RWID account permissions](../01_Core_Chain/rwid-accounts.md) are documented separately and are not a credit-scoring system.

Source: [official ecosystem FAQ](https://docs.flon.network/fullon-faq/what_are_fullon_ecosystems), retrieved 2026-11-02.
