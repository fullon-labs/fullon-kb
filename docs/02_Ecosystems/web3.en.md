# Web3 ecosystem direction

The official FAQ describes tokenized music economies and participant incentives, encourages transparent trading through Swap or order-book DEXs, and discusses gradually introducing DeFi and PayFi.

These directions do not promise returns, availability of every financial service, or execution of any particular trading pair.
