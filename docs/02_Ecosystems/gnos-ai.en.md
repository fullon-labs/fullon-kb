# Gnos AI

Gnos AI has officially launched at [gnos.ai](https://gnos.ai). It combines AI music creation, sharing and social interaction. Its whitepaper proposes a broader music asset and revenue-sharing ecosystem on FullOn Network.

## Product and ecosystem direction

Whitepaper v0.5.0 describes AI music and lyrics generation, listening and curation, a 12-dimensional acoustic quality assessment, Remix royalties, virtual singers, and NFT/SFT records with programmable settlement. Availability depends on current product interfaces and announcements.

## Get started

1. Visit the [official site](https://gnos.ai) and follow its sign-in or registration instructions.
2. Explore available creation, discovery or social features; check displayed fees, quotas and permissions.
3. Before wallet signatures, verify the network, account and authorization. See [HuFu Wallet](hufu-wallet.md).

## GNOS, CISUM and governance

The whitepaper defines GNOS as a governance token and CISUM as non-transferable utility credits. Issuance, redemption, buybacks and DAO mechanisms are whitepaper designs; product launch alone does not confirm their availability. Project governance is separate from FullOn network governance.
